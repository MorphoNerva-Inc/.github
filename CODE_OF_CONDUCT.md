# Code of Conduct

MorphoNerva adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) for all of its public spaces, including repositories, issues and discussions under the MorphoNerva-Inc organization.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, give and accept constructive technical criticism, and don't harass anyone.

**Reporting:** to report a concern, open an issue with the [collaboration form](https://github.com/MorphoNerva-Inc/.github/issues/new?template=collaboration.yml), choose "Other", and ask for a private follow-up. Don't include details of the incident in the public form. A dedicated private contact address will be added here soon. Reports are handled confidentially by company leadership.
