# Contributing to MorphoNerva

Thanks for your interest. MorphoNerva is an early-stage R&D company. Most engineering work currently happens in private repositories, so contribution starts with a conversation.

## How to get involved

1. **Tell us about yourself and your interest** using the [collaboration form](https://github.com/MorphoNerva-Inc/.github/issues/new?template=collaboration.yml). It is public, so please keep it non-confidential.
2. If there is a fit, we follow up privately. Access to private work requires a signed agreement covering confidentiality and intellectual-property assignment.
3. When we publish open-source tools or benchmarks here, each repository will have its own contribution guide and licence.

## Areas where we welcome help

- Spiking neural network training and evaluation
- Digital design (SystemVerilog), verification and formal methods
- FPGA implementation, timing and power measurement
- Compilers, runtimes and developer tooling
- Signal processing for audio, vibration and event-camera data

## What we expect

- Rigour: pre-registered experiments, strong baselines, honest reporting of negative results
- Clean provenance: only contribute work you have the right to contribute. Don't bring code, data or results that belong to an employer, university or lab without written permission
- Respectful collaboration, per our [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
